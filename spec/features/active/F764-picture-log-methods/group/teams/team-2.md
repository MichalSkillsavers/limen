Method: The OODA loop (Observe, Orient, Decide, Act; John Boyd).

Hypothesis: The log is clearest when each entry says where it sits in the decision cycle. An observation (what we saw) leads to orientation (what it means), then to a decision, then to an action. The first view shows open loops: decisions that wait, with their newest observation. Closed loops fold.
